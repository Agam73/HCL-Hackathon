## Fulfilment Options

Every order is fulfilled as either **Home Delivery** or **In-Store Pickup**. Pickup orders are held
at the selected store for 72 hours and are released back to stock afterwards; the customer is
refunded in full.

## Standard Delivery

| Zone | Timeline | Fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Metro (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai) | 1-2 working days | Free above ₹499 |
| Tier-2 cities | 2-4 working days | ₹49 |
| Other serviceable pin codes | 4-7 working days | ₹99 |

Orders below ₹499 in metro zones carry a ₹49 fee.

## Express Delivery

Express delivery (same day where ordered before 12:00, otherwise next day) costs ₹149 and is
available in metro zones only. **Gold and Platinum members receive express delivery free of
charge**, with no minimum order value. Silver members receive it free above ₹2,499. Standard members
pay the full express fee.

## Large Appliance Delivery and Installation

Refrigerators, washing machines and air conditioners are delivered on a scheduled slot and include
free standard installation. Air-conditioner installation includes up to 3 metres of copper piping;
additional material is chargeable. Installation is scheduled within 48 hours of delivery.

## Out-of-Stock After Ordering

If an item becomes unavailable after the order is confirmed, NovaMart offers, in order of
preference: fulfilment from another store or warehouse at no extra cost, a substitute of equal or
higher specification at the original price, or a full refund within 3 working days. The customer is
notified within 4 hours of the shortfall being detected.

## Delivery Attempts

Three attempts are made. After a third failed attempt the order is returned to origin and refunded
minus the shipping fee. Rescheduling is free up to twice from the app.
