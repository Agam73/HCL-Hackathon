## Tiers

Membership is determined by trailing 12-month spend and is reviewed monthly.

| Tier | Qualifying spend (12 months) |
| --- | --- |
| Standard | default on registration |
| Silver | ₹25,000 |
| Gold | ₹75,000 |
| Platinum | ₹2,00,000 |

## Benefit Matrix

| Benefit | Standard | Silver | Gold | Platinum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reward points per ₹100 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 5 |
| Free standard delivery | above ₹499 | above ₹499 | always | always |
| Free express delivery | no | above ₹2,499 | **yes** | **yes** |
| Return window | 7 days | 10 days | 15 days | 15 days |
| Dedicated support queue | no | no | yes | yes, with a named manager |
| Early access to sales | no | 6 hours | 24 hours | 48 hours |
| Free installation on large appliances | yes | yes | yes | yes |
| Annual extended-warranty credit | no | no | ₹1,000 | ₹3,000 |

Gold and Platinum members therefore do receive free express delivery, on any order value.

## Reward Points

One point equals ₹0.25 at checkout. Points post 7 days after delivery, expire 12 months after they
are earned, and cannot be redeemed on gift cards or clearance products. A maximum of 20% of an order
value may be paid with points.

## Tier Downgrade

A tier is retained for 3 months after qualifying spend falls below the threshold, after which the
account moves to the tier its trailing spend supports. Platinum is retained for 6 months.
