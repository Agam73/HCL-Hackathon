## Standard Return Window

Products purchased on novamart.in may be returned within **7 days** of delivery, except where a
category-specific window applies below. The item must include all original accessories, manuals,
freebies and the invoice.

## Opened Electronics

Mobile phones, laptops, tablets and smartwatches may be returned **after being opened** only if the
device is defective, damaged on arrival, or materially different from the listing. An opened device
returned for a change of mind is **not** eligible; an exchange for the same model is offered instead
where the seal has been broken but the device is unused.

Serial numbers are verified at the time of pickup. A device with physical damage, liquid damage or a
removed IMEI label is rejected.

## Large Appliances

Refrigerators, washing machines and air conditioners carry a **10 day** return window from the date
of installation, not the date of delivery. Installation must be performed by a NovaMart-authorised
technician; self-installation voids the return window.

## Discounted and Clearance Products

A product bought on discount is returned at the **price actually paid**, not the list price. Products
marked *Clearance* or *Final Sale* on the product page are not returnable. A discounted product
purchased online **can** be returned at a physical NovaMart store, provided the store stocks that
product category and the order is presented in the NovaMart app.

## Non-Returnable Categories

Groceries, beverages, innerwear, opened personal-care items and digital gift cards are not
returnable.

## Refund Timelines

| Payment method | Refund credited within |
| --- | --- |
| UPI / Net banking | 3 working days |
| Credit / debit card | 5-7 working days |
| NovaMart wallet | instant |
| Cash on delivery | 7 working days to a nominated bank account |
