## Scope

Warranty is provided by the brand, not by NovaMart. NovaMart facilitates pickup, drop-off and
escalation. The warranty period starts on the **invoice date**.

## Standard Periods by Category

| Category | Warranty |
| --- | --- |
| Mobile phones | 12 months (6 months on in-box accessories) |
| Laptops | 12-24 months depending on model |
| Televisions | 12 months, 24 months on the panel for OLED models |
| Refrigerators | 12 months comprehensive + 10 years on the compressor |
| Washing machines | 24 months comprehensive + 10 years on the motor |
| Air conditioners | 12 months comprehensive + 5 years on the compressor |
| Audio and accessories | 6-12 months |
| Footwear | 3 months against manufacturing defects |

A refrigerator therefore carries **one year of comprehensive cover and ten years on the
compressor**. The compressor cover includes the part only; gas charging and labour are chargeable
after the first year.

## What Is Not Covered

Physical damage, liquid ingress, damage from voltage fluctuation without a stabiliser where the
manual requires one, unauthorised repair, consumables (filters, belts, batteries after 6 months),
and cosmetic wear.

## Extended Warranty

An extended warranty may be purchased within 30 days of invoice, adding 12 or 24 months after the
brand warranty ends. It is non-transferable unless the product is gifted through the NovaMart
app.

## Claiming

Raise a ticket in the app or at any store with the invoice and the product serial number. On-site
service applies to large appliances and televisions above 43 inches; all other categories are
carry-in or courier pickup. First response is within 24 hours and resolution targets 72 hours in
serviceable pin codes.
