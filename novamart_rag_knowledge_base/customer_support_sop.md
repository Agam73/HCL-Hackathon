## 1. Purpose and scope
This SOP describes how customer questions and unresolved retail issues are categorized, recorded, and escalated. It supports consistent routing across stores and digital channels. The ticketing system is the source of truth for live ticket status.

## 2. Ticket categories
- **RETURNS_REFUNDS:** Return eligibility, inspection disputes, refund status, or return shipping.
- **WARRANTY_REPAIR:** Warranty coverage, product faults, repair appointments, or service delays.
- **DELIVERY:** Late, failed, damaged, or missing deliveries and tracking discrepancies.
- **INVENTORY_STOCK:** Store stock discrepancies, unexpected stockouts, and availability mismatches.
- **PROMOTIONS_MEMBERSHIP:** Coupon eligibility, membership benefits, or delivery-fee disputes.
- **PRODUCT_GUIDANCE:** Setup, troubleshooting, and product-manual questions.
- **SAFETY:** Reports of overheating, smoke, electrical damage, or other potential product hazards.
- **ACCOUNT_PRIVACY:** Account access, suspected unauthorized access, or personal-data concerns.

## 3. Information to collect
Collect only the information needed to handle the issue:
- Customer's authenticated account or approved contact reference.
- Order number or ticket reference where applicable.
- Product identifier and model when relevant.
- Issue category, concise description, and date observed.
- Relevant evidence such as tracking reference, error code, or photographs where appropriate.
- Actions already taken and the requested resolution.

Never request passwords, full payment-card details, or one-time authentication codes in a support ticket.

## 4. Initial handling
1. Confirm the customer's question and identify the correct category.
2. Check approved policy documents for policy questions.
3. Check authorized order, inventory, or ticket systems for live operational status.
4. Explain only what verified evidence supports.
5. Create or update a ticket when the issue requires human action.
6. Provide the ticket reference and explain the next step without promising an unapproved outcome.

## 5. Escalation rules
- **Policy ambiguity or conflicting versions:** Escalate to the policy owner or Customer Experience Operations.
- **Refund dispute or return-inspection dispute:** Escalate to the Returns Team.
- **Warranty coverage or repeat product failure:** Escalate to Product Service.
- **Missing parcel, repeated failed delivery, or tracking conflict:** Escalate to Fulfilment Support.
- **Store stock mismatch or recurring stockout:** Escalate to the Store Manager and Inventory Operations.
- **Promotion or membership entitlement dispute:** Escalate to Membership and Promotions Support.
- **Suspected account compromise or privacy incident:** Route immediately through the designated Security/Privacy incident process.
- **Potential immediate safety hazard:** Advise the customer to stop using the affected product if safe to do so and route promptly to the Product Safety/Service team. For imminent danger, the customer should contact local emergency services.

## 6. Priority guidance
- **P1 — Urgent:** Immediate safety concerns, suspected account compromise, or a significant active privacy incident. Use the incident process immediately.
- **P2 — High:** A time-sensitive order issue, repeated failed delivery, or a widespread service disruption requiring prompt team action.
- **P3 — Normal:** Routine return, warranty, membership, product-guidance, or stock inquiry.
- **P4 — Informational:** General questions answered completely by an approved knowledge source.

These demo priorities guide routing only. The live support system's approved severity rules take precedence.

## 7. Response and resolution integrity
Do not claim a refund was issued, a ticket was closed, inventory was reserved, or a replacement was approved unless the relevant system confirms the action. The RAG assistant can explain documented policy but cannot perform operational actions merely by generating text.

## 8. Ticket closure
Before closing a ticket, record the resolution, actions taken, relevant approval or transaction reference, customer communication, and any follow-up requirement. Reopen or escalate the case if the reported issue remains unresolved.

## 9. Privacy and audit
Use role-based access, disclose only information necessary for the case, and record material changes. Follow the organization's approved retention and privacy requirements. Do not place sensitive personal data into the general RAG knowledge base.
