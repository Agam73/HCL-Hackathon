## 1. Purpose and scope
This policy explains how customers can return eligible products at participating NovaMart stores, including eligible orders originally placed online. The Online Return Policy defines the standard return window and product-condition rules. This document defines the store process.

## 2. Return window and eligibility
Eligible products must be presented or registered for return within 14 calendar days after delivery for online orders, or within 14 calendar days after purchase for in-store purchases. The delivery or purchase date is day zero. Product-specific exclusions and applicable legal rights still apply.

## 3. Proof of purchase
Customers should provide the order confirmation, receipt, or order number and the account or contact details associated with the purchase. Store staff may use these details to locate the transaction. A bank statement alone may not contain enough information to identify the exact product or order.

## 4. Online orders returned to a store
An online order may be returned at a participating store if:
- The product is eligible under the Online Return Policy.
- The store can identify the order and verify the product.
- The product can be safely accepted and handled by that location.
- Any required return authorization has been obtained.

Large appliances, products requiring specialist handling, and products with special transport requirements may need a scheduled collection rather than an immediate store drop-off. Staff must explain the available process and must not promise that every store accepts every product.

## 5. Store inspection
Staff should compare the item and accessories with the order record, record visible damage, and follow the approved inspection checklist. Staff must not erase customer data without permission. For electronics, customers should sign out and remove personal data before handover where possible.

## 6. Refund processing
For an approved return, the store records the decision and initiates the refund to the original payment method. The target processing time is 5–7 business days after approval; payment providers may take additional time. A store employee must not promise immediate cash or a different payment method unless the authorized system explicitly permits it.

## 7. Damaged, defective, or incorrect items
Staff should record the reported issue and route the case for the appropriate return, replacement, repair, or refund decision. If the issue cannot be resolved at the store, create a Customer Support ticket and provide the customer with its reference number.

## 8. Exceptions and escalation
Escalate cases involving missing order records, disputed inspections, suspected fraud, expired windows, high-value items, or conflicting policy records to the Store Manager or Customer Support. Staff must not override a policy based only on an unverified verbal claim.

## 9. Store procedure
1. Locate the order or receipt.
2. Check the applicable return window and product eligibility.
3. Inspect the item and record condition.
4. Confirm whether the store can accept the product.
5. Record the return decision and provide a receipt or case reference.
6. Initiate the approved refund or explain the next step.
