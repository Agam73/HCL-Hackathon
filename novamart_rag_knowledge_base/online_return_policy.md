## 1. Purpose and scope
This policy describes return eligibility for products purchased through NovaMart's Indian e-commerce channel. It applies to online orders unless a product-specific rule or applicable law provides a stronger customer right. The separate In-Store Return Policy explains how eligible online purchases can be returned at a physical store.

## 2. Standard return window
Eligible products may be returned within 14 calendar days after delivery. The delivery date is day zero; the following calendar day is day one. A return request must be submitted before the end of day 14. Submitting a request within the window preserves the request date while NovaMart reviews eligibility.

## 3. Product condition and packaging
Products should be returned with all supplied accessories, manuals, and original packaging where reasonably available. Normal inspection needed to assess a product does not by itself make it ineligible. Products must not show damage caused by misuse, unauthorized modification, or avoidable mishandling after delivery.

## 4. Opened electronics
Mobile phones, laptops, tablets, and other electronics may be returned within the standard 14-day window if the product is complete and has no customer-caused damage. Before return, customers should sign out of personal accounts, remove screen locks where possible, and erase personal data. Opening a box alone does not automatically disqualify an electronics return. Consumables, software activation codes, and products marked non-returnable at purchase are excluded unless defective or a legal requirement applies.

## 5. Damaged, defective, or incorrect items
Customers should report damage, defects, or an incorrect item as soon as reasonably possible and provide order details and, when useful, photographs. NovaMart may offer a return, replacement, repair, or refund depending on the issue, product type, stock availability, and applicable law. The 14-day standard window does not reduce any separate warranty rights.

## 6. Exclusions
Unless defective or otherwise required by law, the following are not eligible for change-of-mind returns:
- Digital codes or software licenses after activation or redemption.
- Personal-care or hygiene products whose sealed hygiene packaging has been opened.
- Products explicitly marked final sale or non-returnable before purchase.
- Items damaged through misuse, unauthorized repair, or accidental damage after delivery.

## 7. Refund method and timing
After an approved return is received and inspected, NovaMart initiates the refund to the original payment method. The target processing time is 5–7 business days after approval. The bank or payment provider may take additional time to post the credit. If the original payment method is unavailable, Customer Support will explain the available lawful alternatives; customers must not be promised a specific alternative before confirmation.

## 8. Return shipping
For a change-of-mind return, the customer may be responsible for return shipping where this was disclosed at purchase. For a confirmed wrong, damaged, or defective item, NovaMart arranges or reimburses reasonable return shipping according to the approved case resolution and applicable law.

## 9. How to request a return
1. Open the order in the NovaMart account and choose “Request return,” or contact Customer Support.
2. Select the reason and provide the requested details.
3. Wait for the return instructions or approval.
4. Pack the product securely and follow the provided shipping or store-return instructions.
5. Retain the return tracking number or store receipt until the case is closed.

## 10. Escalation and source of truth
Questions about exceptions, disputed inspections, or conflicting policy versions must be referred to Customer Support. This document is demo policy data and must not be represented as a real company's binding policy.
