## 1. Purpose and scope
This demo policy describes NovaMart's limited warranty process for eligible electronics and home appliances sold in India. Manufacturer warranties may provide different or additional coverage. The warranty statement supplied with the product and applicable law take precedence where they provide stronger rights.

## 2. Standard demo warranty periods
Unless the product listing, warranty card, or manufacturer terms state otherwise:
- Smartphones and tablets: 12 months from the purchase date.
- Laptops: 12 months from the purchase date.
- Refrigerators: 24 months for the main appliance and 10 years for the sealed compressor part only, subject to the manufacturer's stated terms.
- Washing machines: 24 months for the main appliance.

These durations are synthetic demonstration values, not verified product guarantees. A customer-specific answer requires the exact product model and applicable warranty terms.

## 3. Covered defects
Subject to the applicable warranty terms, coverage may include defects in materials or workmanship under normal domestic use. A service assessment is required to determine whether a reported fault is covered.

## 4. Exclusions
Unless applicable law or the product's warranty terms provide otherwise, the limited warranty does not cover:
- Accidental damage, drops, liquid ingress, or misuse.
- Normal wear and cosmetic damage that does not result from a manufacturing defect.
- Damage from unauthorized repair, modification, or incompatible accessories.
- Consumables and accessories not expressly included in the warranty.
- Faults caused by unsuitable power supply, improper installation, or failure to follow the product manual.

## 5. Proof of purchase and claim process
1. Locate the receipt, invoice, or order confirmation.
2. Record the product model, serial number, purchase date, and fault symptoms.
3. Contact NovaMart Customer Support or the authorized service channel stated in the product documentation.
4. Follow instructions for diagnosis, collection, or service-center delivery.
5. Keep the service reference number and any handover receipt.
6. Wait for the service assessment and decision.

## 6. Repair, replacement, and costs
Depending on the applicable terms and service assessment, the resolution may be repair, replacement, or another remedy required by law. Do not promise a particular resolution before the authorized service team has assessed the claim. Transport, labor, and parts charges depend on the applicable warranty terms and the cause of failure.

## 7. Warranty versus return
The standard change-of-mind return window is 14 calendar days under the Online Return Policy. A warranty claim is a separate process and may remain available after that return window. Expiration of the return window does not, by itself, determine whether a warranty claim is valid.

## 8. Escalation
Escalate disputes about coverage, missing warranty records, repeat failures, or safety-related faults to the Product Service Team. Stop using a product if continued operation may create a safety risk and follow the manufacturer's safety instructions.
