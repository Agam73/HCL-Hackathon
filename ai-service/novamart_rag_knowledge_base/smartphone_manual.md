## 1. Scope and safety
This generic demo manual describes basic setup and troubleshooting for a fictional NovaMart smartphone. Button placement, charging specifications, menus, and reset behavior vary by model. Do not use this document as a substitute for the exact manufacturer's manual.

## 2. Initial setup
1. Inspect the phone and supplied accessories for visible shipping damage.
2. Charge the phone using a compatible charger and cable specified by the manufacturer.
3. Power on the device using its power button.
4. Follow the on-screen language, network, account, and security setup steps.
5. Install system updates over a trusted network when prompted.
6. Keep the device serial number and proof of purchase for support or warranty claims.

## 3. Charging
Use a compatible, undamaged charger and cable. Keep charging ports dry and free of debris. Do not charge a device that is swollen, unusually hot, visibly damaged, or wet. Disconnect it if it emits smoke, a burning smell, or unusual sounds, and seek qualified service. Do not insert metal objects into the charging port.

## 4. Connectivity troubleshooting
If Wi-Fi does not connect:
1. Confirm that Wi-Fi is enabled and the correct password is entered.
2. Check whether other devices can connect to the same network.
3. Toggle Wi-Fi off and on.
4. Restart the phone and router if appropriate.
5. If the issue persists across networks, record the error and contact support.

For mobile-network problems, confirm that airplane mode is off, the SIM or eSIM is active, and the location has network coverage. Carrier-specific issues may require the mobile operator.

## 5. Restarting the device
Save open work and use the on-screen power menu to restart the phone. If the screen is unresponsive, consult the exact model's official manual for its hardware-button restart procedure. Button combinations differ by model.

## 6. Factory reset and data protection
A factory reset erases local user data and settings. Before resetting:
- Back up important data.
- Confirm that account credentials and multi-factor recovery methods are available.
- Remove or back up files that are not synced.
- Follow the device's official reset instructions.

Use the device settings menu's reset function when accessible. If the phone is managed by an employer or another organization, contact the administrator first. A reset may not remove activation locks or organization management.

## 7. Common symptoms
- **Phone will not power on:** Try a known-compatible charger and cable, allow a reasonable charging period, and inspect for visible damage. Seek service if there is no response.
- **Phone overheats:** Stop demanding tasks, disconnect charging if safe, move the device away from heat, and let it cool. Do not put it in a refrigerator or freezer.
- **Storage is full:** Review storage settings, back up important files, and remove content only when you are sure it is no longer needed.
- **Apps crash:** Update the app and operating system, restart the device, and contact the app provider if the issue persists.

## 8. Support and warranty
Keep the purchase receipt, device model, serial number, and a description of the symptoms. For hardware defects, consult the NovaMart Warranty Policy and contact the authorized support channel. Do not attempt unauthorized repairs.
