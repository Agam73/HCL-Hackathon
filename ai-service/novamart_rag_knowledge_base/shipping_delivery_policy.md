## 1. Purpose and scope
This policy describes standard and express home delivery for eligible NovaMart orders in supported Indian service areas. Delivery estimates are estimates, not guaranteed arrival times, unless an explicit guaranteed service is confirmed at checkout.

## 2. Standard delivery
Standard delivery typically takes 3–7 business days after dispatch in supported service areas. Remote locations, public holidays, severe weather, carrier disruptions, and product-specific handling can extend the estimate. The delivery estimate shown for a specific order is the best available order-level reference.

## 3. Express delivery
Express delivery typically targets 1–2 business days after dispatch where the service is offered and the item is eligible. Express service may not be available for large appliances, oversized products, restricted items, remote postal codes, or orders that require special handling. Eligibility and the final fee must be shown or confirmed at checkout.

## 4. Demo delivery fees and membership benefits
For demonstration purposes, standard delivery costs ₹99 per order and express delivery costs ₹199 per order when available. Gold and Platinum members receive free standard delivery on eligible orders. Platinum members may receive free express delivery when the order and destination qualify. Silver members receive standard delivery at the displayed checkout fee; Standard members receive no automatic delivery-fee waiver.

These synthetic fee and benefit rules are aligned with the demo Membership Benefits data. The checkout system remains authoritative for an actual order's fee and eligibility.

## 5. Supported service regions
Delivery is available only to postal codes supported by NovaMart's delivery service. The presence of a store in a city does not prove that every postal code in that city is serviceable. Customers should enter their delivery postal code at checkout to verify availability and estimated delivery.

## 6. Dispatch and tracking
Customers receive order and tracking information when it becomes available. “Processing,” “dispatched,” and “out for delivery” are different statuses. The assistant must not infer a live order status from this policy document; live status must come from the order service.

## 7. Delays and failed delivery
If delivery is delayed beyond the displayed estimate, customers should check the tracking record and contact Customer Support if the tracking information is unavailable or unchanged for an extended period. After an unsuccessful delivery attempt, the carrier may arrange another attempt or request updated delivery instructions.

## 8. Cancellation and address changes
Cancellation or address changes depend on order status and fulfilment constraints. A request may no longer be possible after dispatch. The order system or Customer Support must confirm whether the change is available.

## 9. Escalation
Escalate repeated failed delivery attempts, missing parcels, damaged deliveries, and tracking records that conflict with customer reports to the Fulfilment Support Team. Do not claim that a parcel has been delivered or is in transit without current order-system evidence.
