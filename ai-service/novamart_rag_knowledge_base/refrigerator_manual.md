## 1. Scope and safety
This is a generic demonstration manual. Control labels and error codes differ between refrigerator models. Use the exact model manual for model-specific settings. Do not remove electrical covers or attempt repairs involving refrigerant or live electrical components.

## 2. Temperature settings
For this demo manual, a recommended starting setting is approximately 3–4 °C for the refrigerator compartment and -18 °C for the freezer. Allow the appliance time to stabilize after changing settings or loading a large quantity of room-temperature food. Use a suitable appliance thermometer if temperature accuracy is in doubt. Follow the manufacturer's guidance for the exact model.

## 3. Placement and ventilation
Place the refrigerator on a stable, level surface and leave the clearances specified by the manufacturer. Keep ventilation openings unobstructed. Avoid placing the appliance beside a strong heat source or in direct sunlight where this conflicts with installation guidance.

## 4. Food storage and door use
Keep doors closed as much as possible and ensure that door seals are clean and undamaged. Avoid blocking internal air vents. Allow hot food to cool safely before storage according to food-safety guidance. If food has been held at unsafe temperatures, do not assume it is safe merely because the refrigerator later returns to its normal setting.

## 5. Cleaning and maintenance
1. Follow the manufacturer's instructions and disconnect power before any cleaning that requires it.
2. Use a soft cloth and a suitable mild cleaning solution.
3. Do not use abrasive tools or pour water onto electrical components.
4. Clean door seals gently and check that they close properly.
5. Keep external ventilation areas free of dust.
6. Do not chip ice with sharp tools or attempt to handle refrigerant lines.

## 6. Demo troubleshooting codes
The following codes are fictional examples for demonstrating RAG retrieval. They are not universal refrigerator error codes.

- **E1 — Demo meaning: temperature sensor reading unavailable.** Check whether the displayed code remains after consulting the exact model manual. If it persists, contact authorized service.
- **E2 — Demo meaning: door may not be closing correctly.** Check for obstructing items and inspect whether the door seal is visibly dirty or blocked. Do not dismantle the door.
- **E3 — Demo meaning: cooling performance warning.** Check that ventilation openings are clear and the door is closed. If the appliance remains warm or the warning persists, move perishable food to safe storage and contact authorized service.

Never map these demo codes to a real appliance without verification against its manufacturer documentation.

## 7. When to stop and seek service
Stop using the appliance and seek qualified help if there is a burning smell, electrical sparking, damaged power cable, suspected refrigerant leak, or another immediate safety concern. Do not attempt electrical or sealed-system repairs yourself.

## 8. Warranty and service
Record the model number, serial number, purchase date, displayed code, and symptoms. Consult the NovaMart Warranty Policy for the demo claim process and contact an authorized service channel. Warranty eligibility depends on the exact product terms and service assessment.
