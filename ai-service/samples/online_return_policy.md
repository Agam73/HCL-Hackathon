# Online Return Policy (synthetic demo)

This document is a **synthetic NovaMart demo policy** for hackathon evaluation.
It is not a real corporate policy.

## Opened Electronics

Opened mobile phones purchased online in region IN may be returned within 7
calendar days of delivery if the device has no physical damage and all
accessories are included. A 10 percent restocking fee applies to GOLD and
standard membership tiers. The original payment method is refunded after
inspection.

## Unopened Electronics

Unopened electronics in original sealed packaging may be returned within 15
calendar days of delivery with no restocking fee.

## Clothing

Clothing purchased online may be returned within 30 calendar days if unused
and with tags attached.

## What is not covered

Personalized items, gift cards, and software download codes cannot be returned.
This policy does not describe warranty repairs. Warranty duration is documented
in the separate Product Warranty Guide.
